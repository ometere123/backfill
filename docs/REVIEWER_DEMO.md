# Reviewer demo path

1. Connect a wallet and switch it to Studionet.
2. Open the current epoch and fund its pool with GEN.
3. Submit a real, pre-existing public contribution URL.
4. Evaluate the claim and show its finalized lifecycle plus grounded evidence.
5. Open the bounded challenge route if counter-evidence exists.
6. Finalize the epoch after the windows close, then finalize the pool.
7. Claim GEN and show the receipt, explorer link and authoritative reread.
8. Show an ineligible or inconclusive claim with zero weight.

Live contract addresses and transaction IDs must be filled only from actual Studionet receipts.
